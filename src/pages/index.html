    Before you buy

    A holiday home comes with a pitch, and the pitch comes with an agreement with the park. Ask every seller for the current site fees, how many years the park will let the home stay, and whether the sale includes transfer of the pitch. Our buyer’s guide lists the questions worth asking, and buying safely covers how to avoid scams.
