Place a free advert

For private owners selling their own holiday home. It takes about ten minutes and costs nothing. Dealers and parks, please use trade advertising.

The home
Is it a caravan or a lodge?
For example: “2019 two-bed caravan with sea views and decking”
Features
Condition, recent work, what’s included, and why you’re selling. Buyers like detail.
The park and pitch
Is the home on a pitch?
Price and photos

Up to six photos, JPG or PNG. Keep the total under 8MB — photos straight from a phone are usually 2–4MB each, so you may need to send three now and email the rest.

Your details

Your email is never shown on the advert. We forward buyers’ enquiries to you.

What happens next

We read every advert before it goes live, normally within one working day. We’ll email you when it’s published, or if we need anything else.

Your advert stays up until you tell us it’s sold. To change the price or details, reply to our email.

Adverts that sell

  • A bright outside photo first
  • Site fees and season length stated
  • How many years the park allows
  • Honest about any wear
