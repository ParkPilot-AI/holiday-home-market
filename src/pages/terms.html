Advertising terms

Last updated 31 October 2026. Plain-English terms for everyone who advertises with us.

These terms are a starting template. Have them checked by a solicitor before you rely on them, and add your company name and registered address.

1. Who we are

Holiday Home Market (“we”, “us”) runs this website as an advertising service. We are not a party to any sale and we don’t act as an agent for buyers or sellers.

2. Private adverts

Private adverts are free and are only for individuals selling a holiday home they own. One home per advert. If we believe an advertiser is a business, we may remove the advert or ask them to list as trade.

3. Trade adverts

Businesses pay £5 per home listed. Payment is taken by card through Stripe before the listing is published. The fee covers the listing until the home is sold or withdrawn. If we decline to publish a listing we refund it in full; fees for published listings are not refundable.

4. Your responsibilities

  • Your advert must be accurate and you must have the right to sell the home.
  • Photos must be your own or used with permission.
  • You must tell us promptly when the home is sold or no longer available.
  • You must deal with buyers honestly and comply with the law, including consumer protection law for trade sellers.

5. What we can refuse or remove

We review every advert and may edit, refuse or remove any advert that is misleading, duplicated, unlawful, or not a static caravan or lodge, without giving a reason.

6. Liability

We don’t inspect homes or verify advertisers and we aren’t responsible for the condition of any home, the conduct of any buyer or seller, or the outcome of any sale. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

7. Law

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.
