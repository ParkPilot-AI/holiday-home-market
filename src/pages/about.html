About Holiday Home Market

A place for holiday home owners to sell directly to the next family, without paying to do it.

Selling a static caravan or lodge usually means part-exchanging it with the park or a dealer, often for much less than it’s worth to a private buyer. Classified sites that cover everything bury holiday homes among cars and sofas, and specialist sites charge owners to advertise.

We started Holiday Home Market to do one thing well: let owners advertise their holiday home free, in front of people looking for exactly that.

How we pay for it

Dealers and parks pay £5 for each home they list. That keeps private adverts free, with no adverts for other products cluttering the page.

What we don’t do

We don’t buy, sell, value or inspect homes, and we never handle money between buyers and sellers. We’re an advertising board, run by people who read every advert before it goes up.
