Privacy notice

Last updated 31 October 2026.

What we collect

When you place an advert, send an enquiry or contact us, we collect what you type into the form: your name, email, phone number if given, and the details and photos of your home.

How we use it

  • To publish and manage your advert
  • To forward buyers’ enquiries to sellers
  • To take payment for trade listings (card payments are handled by Stripe; we never see card numbers)
  • To reply to messages

Our lawful basis is performing our agreement with you, and our legitimate interest in running a safe advertising service.

What’s public

The advert text and photos are public. Your email address is never shown. Your phone number is shown only if you choose.

Who we share it with

Our hosting and form provider (Netlify), our payment provider (Stripe), and the seller when you send an enquiry. We don’t sell your data.

How long we keep it

We keep advert details for 12 months after the advert ends, then delete them. Payment records are kept for six years for tax purposes.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by emailing hello@holidayhomemarket.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies. The site stores a small amount of information in your browser during a trade payment, which is removed when you close the tab.
